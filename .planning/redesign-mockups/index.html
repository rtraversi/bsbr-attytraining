Iurix Accreditation · marketing redesign

Three directions

Same copy and section structure in all three, taken from the retired Netlify site — with the "coming soon" and waitlist sections replaced by the real checkout CTA. Every colour is sampled from public/brand/iurix-logo.svg; every typeface is already self-hosted in public/fonts/, so whichever you pick ships without new licensing.

A

Marble & Rule

Light and editorial. The certificate as a printed instrument — marble ground, hairline rules, serif display, rose gold reserved for the seal and the one primary action. Calm authority.

 B

Deep Water

Dark jewel. Near-black teal so the metallic mark reads the way it was drawn. Very large, very light display type, lots of air, rose gold used only for actions. Premium-software register.

 C

The Record

The deliverable is the hero — a certificate specimen sits in the first screen. Systematic and gridded, mono metadata, filled teal bands, a tabular sanctions docket. Unmistakably compliance.
