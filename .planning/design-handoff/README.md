## What this is

A self-contained brief for redesigning the **marketing surface** of Iurix Accreditation — a live,
working SaaS product. The application behind it (checkout, auth, training, certification,
dashboards) is **already built and deployed**. Nothing in this pack asks you to design or rebuild
that.

Read the files in order:

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **`01-brief.md`** | The product, the buyer, the job the site has to do, and what's in / out of scope |
| **`02-brand.md`** | Palette, typography, logo assets, and the one hard brand constraint |
| **`03-copy.md`** | The actual approved copy, section by section. Use it verbatim |
| **`04-tech-constraints.md`** | The stack you're designing into, and the integration points you cannot break |

## The three reference mockups

`reference/` contains three complete homepage directions built in-house. Open them in a browser.

**They are not a spec and you are not being asked to pick one.** They exist so you can see the
palette applied at full page scale and understand the register we're aiming at. If your own
direction is better, propose it — that's why you're being engaged.

They will render in fallback fonts until you run the font-copy step in `assets/fonts/README.md`.

---

## The single most important instruction

**This is a compliance product bought by lawyers.** The buyer is a solo attorney or small-firm
partner who is personally, professionally liable for what their staff do with AI. They are not
looking to be delighted. They are looking to be *reassured that this is real and defensible*.

Design for credibility over novelty. If a choice makes the site look more like a serious
professional instrument and less like a startup landing page, make that choice.
