## 1. Who We Are and Our Role

Iurix provides training and certification services directly to law firms and to the individual staff
members those firms enrol.

**Unlike a practice management or case management platform, Iurix does not hold your clients' data.**
The Service contains no case files, no client records, no immigration or litigation documents, and no
attorney work product. Nothing stored in Iurix is subject to attorney-client privilege, because no
privileged material ever enters the Service.

What we hold is a record of *training and certification* — who was enrolled, what they completed,
how they scored, and what certificate was issued. For that data, **Iurix acts as a data controller**:
we determine what is collected and how it is used, because the integrity of a certification record
is the product itself.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Firm and Administrator Information

When a firm purchases seats and an administrator sets up an account, we collect:

- Firm name
- Administrator name and email address
- Billing information, processed by our payment processor. **We never receive or store full payment
  card numbers**
- Subscription details — seat count, billing period, and renewal date

### Staff Member Information

When a firm administrator invites a staff member, we collect:

- Name and email address, as supplied by the administrator

We do **not** collect profile photographs or any other image of a staff member.

> **Important:** staff members are typically enrolled *by their employer*, not by their own choice.
> If you are a staff member and have questions about why you were enrolled, speak to your firm
> administrator in the first instance. You retain the rights described in Section 7 regardless.

### Training and Certification Records

This is the core of what the Service exists to produce. For each enrolled staff member we record:

- Progress through the training content, including which lesson was last reached and total time spent
- Knowledge check and assessment attempts, including the score for each attempt and whether it passed
- Certificate issuance — a unique certificate number, the issue date, and the expiry date
- The generated PDF certificate itself
- **The IP address and browser user-agent captured at the moment of assessment submission**

That last item deserves explanation. When a staff member submits the certifying assessment, they
make an identity attestation — a confirmation that they personally completed the training. We record
the IP address and user-agent alongside that attestation because a certificate that cannot be tied to
a submission event is not credible evidence of supervision. This data exists to make your compliance
record defensible.

### Usage and Log Data

We automatically collect limited operational data, including error reports, performance data, and
request logs generated by our hosting infrastructure.

### Communications

If you contact us for support, we retain a record of that correspondence in order to resolve the
issue and improve our support.

### What We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect client data, case data, or any legal work product
- We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party marketing analytics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provision firm accounts and staff seats
- Deliver the training content and administer the certification assessment
- Score assessments and determine whether a passing standard was met
- Generate, store, and deliver certificates
- Give firm administrators a dashboard showing completion status, scores, and certificate validity
- Send transactional email — invitations, reminders, certificate delivery, and renewal notices
- Process payments and send billing correspondence
- Detect, prevent, and address security incidents or abuse

**We do not sell your information, and we do not sell or share the information of the staff members
you enrol.**

**We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.** See our separate

## 5. Data Retention

Retention here works differently from most software services, and the reason matters.

**Certification records are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years from the date of certificate
issuance.** A certificate is evidence that a supervising attorney took reasonable steps to train
their staff under ABA Model Rule 5.3. That evidence has to survive longer than the subscription that
produced it — a record that vanishes when a firm stops paying is worthless as a compliance
artifact. Retained records include the certificate, the assessment result that produced it, and the
attestation metadata described in Section 2.

Other retention periods:

- **Account and billing records** — retained for the life of the subscription and afterwards as
  required by applicable tax and accounting law
- **Operational logs** — retained on a rolling short-term basis for security and troubleshooting
  `[TBD — specify an exact period]`
- **Support correspondence** — retained for as long as needed to resolve and audit the issue

### When a Staff Member Is Removed

If a firm administrator deletes a staff member, we **redact that person's directly identifying
information** — their email address is replaced with a non-routable placeholder value and their
account is marked deleted — while **preserving the underlying training and certification record**.

This is deliberate, and we want to be plain about it: the firm's compliance evidence survives the
removal of the individual, but the individual is no longer identifiable within it in the ordinary
course. If you need complete erasure of a record rather than redaction, contact us using the details
in Section 10; we will assess the request against our and the firm's retention obligations.

## 6. Data Security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect your data, including:

- **Encryption in transit** — all traffic is served over TLS
- **Encryption at rest** — data is encrypted at the storage layer by our infrastructure providers
- **Tenant isolation enforced at the database layer** — every firm's records are separated by
  row-level security policies, so a query issued on behalf of one firm cannot return another firm's
  rows
- **Role-based access** — administrators and staff members see different data, enforced on the server
  rather than in the browser
- **Private certificate storage** — certificate PDFs are held in private storage and are reachable
  only through short-lived signed links, not public URLs
- **Server-side scoring** — assessments are scored on our servers. A score submitted by a browser is
  never trusted

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data associated with you
- **Correction** — correct inaccurate account information, through the Service or by contacting us

Firm administrators can exercise most of these directly in the dashboard. Staff members should
contact their firm administrator first for account changes, and may contact us directly at any time.
